biotech 01 / THE TMC BIOBANK

Biospecimens & Biobank Facility

Preserving biological samples and health information for research.

science A resource for health research

Established in 2005, the TMC biobank stores biospecimens collected for basic testing and research. UKM describes blood fractions including red blood cells (RBC), mononuclear cells (MNC), plasma, serum, and DNA, alongside whole urine, urine pellets, and faecal samples.

BloodRBC · MNC · plasma · serum · DNA
UrineWhole urine · urine pellets
Faecal samplesListed in UKM’s biobank overview

Basic blood testing described by UKM includes blood glucose, lipid profiles, and kidney profiles.

verified Bioanalytical laboratory accreditation

UKM’s published infographic identifies the Level 6 Bioanalytical Laboratory under the Biobank & Bioanalytical Division (SAMM No. 537). It records renewal and upgrade to MS ISO 15189:2022 on 11 June 2025.

The listed scope covers haematology and chemical pathology, including full blood count, lipid and renal profiles, urine analysis, and HbA1c.

Data & information systemsexpand_more

UKM's information technology overview describes four systems supporting the collection and management of health data and biospecimens.

eCIMS · Cohort information
Manages study information across registration, appointments, measurements, questionnaires, biospecimens, and quality control.
TSIMS · Biospecimen tubes
Records tube inventory and storage locations using 2D barcodes.
DIMS · Diet information
Records food intake and supports analysis of participants' nutrition information.
HeDIMS · Health follow-up
Supports recording disease and treatment information collected during follow-up calls.

Guidelines for Collaboration

Planning research with The Malaysian Cohort’s data and biospecimens.

gavel UKM’s published collaboration guidance

UKM welcomes collaborators from within and outside Malaysia and asks researchers to discuss proposals at an early stage. The points below summarise the guidance published on its research page.

  1. 01

    Discuss the proposal early

    Discuss the research proposal with UMBI at the beginning, before the grant is approved.

  2. 02

    Plan the grant allocation

    UKM’s guidelines require a portion of the grant to be transferred to UMBI for storage, maintenance, consumables, and rental charges.

  3. 03

    Include UMBI investigators

    Include at least one co-investigator from UMBI, with a significant portion of the work performed at UMBI.

  4. 04

    Include research students

    Each project should have at least two research students; one must be registered and placed under UMBI.

  5. 05

    Agree any specimen transfer

    Biospecimens are normally retained at UMBI. External analysis may be considered when methods or equipment are unavailable locally, or analysis is cheaper elsewhere. A material transfer agreement is required for transfer.

  6. 06

    Coordinate data analysis

    UMBI biostatisticians and data scientists analyse the cohort database and provide relevant tables for reporting or publication.

  7. 07

    Arrange project-specific data

    Where necessary, relevant project data may be placed in a separate file for analysis by an external collaborator.

  8. 08

    Discuss corresponding authorship

    The corresponding author is generally from UMBI. A co-corresponding author from the collaborating institution may be allowed where justified.

  9. 09

    Discuss intellectual property

    UKM’s guidelines state that intellectual property from cohort data and biospecimens belongs to UMBI; shared intellectual property may be discussed case by case.

  10. 10

    Respect resource ownership

    The published guidelines retain UMBI and UKM rights over TMC data, biospecimens, discoveries, and resulting publications.
