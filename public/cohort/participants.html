02 / YOUR SCREENING VISIT

Guidelines to Participants

Undergoing health screening at The Malaysian Cohort Project

The team contacts participants to arrange appointments. UKM describes a screening visit of approximately 3–4 hours, conducted by trained staff.

  1. 01

    Questionnaire session

    Health and treatment history, residence, occupation, and diet.

  2. 02

    Blood & urine tests

    Glucose, lipids/cholesterol, full blood count, HbA1c, and kidney function.

  3. 03

    Biophysical measurements

    Blood pressure, ECG, spirometry, and blood vessel tests.

Arrival, registration & parkingexpand_more

Check your name at the UMBI lobby security counter, then go to Level 6 and present your identity card at registration.

UKM lists limited UMBI parking and nearby paid parking options, including HCTM Zones L and M and DBKL parking near Cheras Football Stadium. Read the full UKM guide for directions and shuttle information.

04 / MEASUREMENTS

Biophysical Measurement

UKM describes the following measurement areas. Refer to the published procedures and the TMC team’s instructions for your visit.

  • AnthropometryHeight, weight, waist and hip measurements.
  • Blood pressureMeasurement of blood pressure.
  • Body compositionBody composition analysis using BIA.
  • DEXABody composition and bone density measurements.
  • Retinal scanImages of the retina.
  • Vascular screeningBlood vessel assessment.
  • SpirometryLung function measurement.
  • Electrocardiogram (ECG)Recording of heart activity.
  • MRIMagnetic resonance imaging information.

05 / YOUR HEALTH & DIET

Questionnaire & Food Frequency Questionnaire (FFQ)

Questionnaire

The session takes approximately 45–60 minutes and covers 14 areas of personal, health, lifestyle, and family information.

Food Frequency Questionnaire

The FFQ records food and drink consumption, frequency, and portion sizes. Participants receive an explanation before completing it.

What does the questionnaire cover?expand_more
  • Demographics
  • Medical background
  • Complementary & alternative medicine
  • Surgical history
  • Immunisation history
  • Blood transfusion
  • Fracture injuries
  • Questions for women
  • Family history
  • Physical activity
  • Environment & occupation
  • Tobacco & related products
  • Alcohol
  • Food consumption habits

06 / SAMPLES FOR RESEARCH

Biospecimen

Blood and urine samples form part of the health screening described by UKM. The TMC biobank stores biospecimens for basic testing and health research.

  • Blood tests include glucose, lipid profile, full blood count, HbA1c, and kidney function.
  • Samples are processed and stored by the TMC laboratory and biobank.

07 / COLORECTAL SCREENING

Colorectal Cancer Screening

UKM describes stool-based screening using an iFOBT (immunochemical faecal occult blood test) kit. The test checks for blood in the stool.

Using the kit

Follow the collection instructions and video published by UKM. Contact TMC for kit availability and return arrangements.

Read kit instructions & FAQsopen_in_new

Understanding the result

A positive iFOBT result is not a diagnosis of cancer. UKM advises further medical assessment and explains the follow-up process in its FAQs.

08 / AFTER YOUR VISIT

Results and Feedback

The Results and Feedback Unit prepares printed screening results for participants. The sheet includes blood, urine, body composition, and biophysical measurement information.

UKM’s published dispatch timeframe14 working days

From recruitment, with results posted through Pos Malaysia to the registered address.

Receiving and discussing your results

  • Body composition results are provided on the screening day, according to the participant guide.
  • Contact TMC if you have questions about delivery.
  • Bring the result sheet to your doctor for professional medical advice.

UKM also describes annual health-status follow-up through telephone interviews.
