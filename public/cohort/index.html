ABOUT THE MALAYSIAN COHORT

A national resource.
A shared purpose.

The Malaysian Cohort brings together long-term health information and biological samples to study how genes, lifestyle, and environment influence disease.

Its research aims to identify earlier signs of disease and support prevention, with a national biobank and collaboration across the scientific community.

Our identity & fieldwork arrow_forwardExplore the biobank arrow_forward

What the study aims to do

  1. 01 / UNDERSTAND

    Study disease risk

    Explore the roles and interactions of genes, environment, and lifestyle.

  2. 02 / DISCOVER

    Find earlier markers

    Use genomic and proteomic research to investigate early signs of cancer and other diseases.

  3. 03 / PRESERVE

    Build a research resource

    Maintain health data and a national biobank for the scientific community.

  4. 04 / CONNECT

    Collaborate for discovery

    Work through international research partnerships and consortia to advance life sciences and biotechnology.

What happens during a screening visit?expand_more

UKM describes a visit of approximately 3–4 hours: questionnaires, blood and urine tests, and biophysical measurements. The team contacts participants to arrange appointments.

Screening visit guide arrow_forward
How will I receive my screening results?expand_more

UKM describes printed results sent through Pos Malaysia to the registered address, with a stated dispatch timeframe of 14 working days from recruitment. Contact TMC about delivery questions.
